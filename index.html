Our Philosophy

"A great fragrance is not worn — it is lived in."

— Mimi Laurent, Founder

Born in Grasse, the perfume capital of the world, Mimi Laurent spent two decades learning the ancient art of fragrance creation from master perfumers. Scent by Mimi is the culmination of that journey — a house dedicated to creating timeless, wearable art that speaks to the senses and touches the soul.

Every bottle is an intimate story. Every spritz, a memory waiting to be made.
